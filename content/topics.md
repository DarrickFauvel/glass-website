# Blog topic queue

Work top to bottom: the next post covers the first unchecked topic. Check a topic off in the same PR as its post. When the list runs out, add 5 new topics in the same spirit (popular claims where the evidence is clear enough to say something useful) before drafting.

The first ten come from the hero-section "clues" in `public/js/main.js`. Each one's belief and question are the suggested angle; a post must answer the question.

- [x] **bigfoot** — *Belief:* Bigfoot is an undiscovered great ape roaming remote North American forests, supported by footprints and footage like the Patterson–Gimlin film. *Question:* Why has no confirmed physical evidence (bones, tissue, or definitive DNA) ever been recovered?
- [x] **chemtrails** — *Belief:* Aircraft trails are chemical or biological agents deliberately sprayed by governments. *Question:* Contrails are well-understood condensation, so how would a program requiring the silence of hundreds of thousands of people across competing nations stay secret?
- [x] **gmos** — *Belief:* Genetically modified organisms pose unique dangers to health and ecosystems, and safety data is suppressed. *Question:* What specific biological mechanism would make a genetically engineered crop inherently harmful?
- [ ] **psychics** — *Belief:* Some people can read minds, predict the future, or talk with the dead. *Question:* Why do psychic powers vanish when double-blind testing rules out cold reading and confirmation bias?
- [ ] **ufos** — *Belief:* Unidentified aerial phenomena are extraterrestrial spacecraft, with recovered craft covered up. *Question:* What evidence distinguishes an unexplained phenomenon from an alien spacecraft?
- [ ] **astrology** — *Belief:* The positions of celestial bodies at birth shape personality and destiny. *Question:* Why do controlled studies find no correlation between birth date and personality?
- [ ] **spirits** — *Belief:* Spirits of the dead can interact with the living or speak through mediums. *Question:* Why do mediums perform at chance when verifiable specifics are required?
- [ ] **aliens** — *Belief:* Extraterrestrials have visited Earth and governments suppress the evidence. *Question:* What testable evidence supports a visit to Earth specifically?
- [ ] **ghosts** — *Belief:* Ghosts linger in the physical world, detectable by cold spots, EVP, or EMF meters. *Question:* What evidence links EMF fluctuations to anything other than wiring and electronics?
- [ ] **flat-earth** — *Belief:* Earth is a flat disc and space agencies hide its true shape. *Question:* What flat-Earth model makes predictions as precise as GPS, eclipses, and circumnavigation?
